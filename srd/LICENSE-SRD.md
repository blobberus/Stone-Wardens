The SRD 5.2 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The files `srd/*.json` (classes, features, subclasses, species, backgrounds, feats, spells, weapons, armor, gear, magic-items, rules) contain SRD 5.2 text. They were generated by `tools/build_srd.py`, which reformats the text as Markdown, restructures it for a character builder, and corrects some values against the SRD 5.2 tables. The script prints every correction it makes when it runs. The Eldritch Invocation and Metamagic option summaries in `features.json` are short paraphrases written for this project, not SRD text.

## Stubs (`srd/stubs/*.json`) are not WotC text

The stub files name official content that is not in the SRD (for example, 2024 Player's Handbook subclasses, backgrounds and feats, and species from older books). They list names, levels and basic mechanical facts, plus short summaries written in our own words for this project. They contain no Wizards of the Coast text, and they do not replace the books. Each stub entry has `"stub": true` and a `source` field naming the book it comes from. Entries marked `"verify": true` have not been checked against the book.

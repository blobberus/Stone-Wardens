**Brief and History:**
The southernmost region part of Caspernian rule. Conquered in 940AFF by King Halstern the Stoic. The land of Caspana was known as Stellanar before the Caspernian conquest annexed and renamed the region to Caspana to honor King Haspan(the father of King Halstern)'s expansion of the [[Kingdom of Caspernos]].  

In the 1100s, Northern Caspana became absorbed into the [[Väng ]] Region under the King's decree--making the remaining territory adopt the name: Southern Caspana.

**Locations:**
The capital of Southern Caspana is Wyvernthor. Smaller towns and settlements are primarily scattered and loosely governed. Being an ancient nation, Caspana holds many ruins dating back to 200AFF, before the Caspernian reign in the region. A hands-off governance has caused lucrative merchant routes but also unsafe crossings infested with gangs and deadly creatures.

**Economy**:
Today, colloquially, Southern Caspana is called the Southern Reach or the Greener Lands due to its lush forests and quiet presence. The people of Southern Caspana are known to be primarily farmers and craftsmen with few remaining warrior groups from the ancient times known as the Wyvern Wing. The reddish-orange wood from the Wyvern Wood (called Wyvernicus) in the region under the careful craftsmanship of local luthiers, carpenters, and blacksmiths is the greatest material export to the Caspernian and Tuldotian merchants crossing the Southern Route. 

**Culture:**
The craft-focused working class of Southern Caspana fostered an artistic culture within Caspaners. Caspaner music, played by various types of Wyvernicus flutes which has a characteristically melancholy yet warm sound is listened to across the Caspernos Kingdom. The region is also known for cloudless nights that boast beautiful stars of the pantheon.

The artistic affect has made Caspaners largely peaceful and welcoming to outsiders. However, a history of invasion from neighboring nations (and current governing [[Kingdom of Caspernos]]) has brewed a devout subculture of warriors known as the Wyvern Wing. 

More minor cultural exports such as Caspaner Cuisine: Simmered Pseudodragon and Fried Giant Spiders; Caspaner Weapons: Wyvernicus Wooden Swords and Headless Polearm.

Religion among Caspaners is devout. Most believe in The Almighty. Sub followers worship Wyvan, the God of Wyvernicus and Clef, the Goddess of Music. Low governance and little war has resulted in a proliferation of religious folk who specialize in the study of a small, often minor belief. 


**Current King**: [[Kingdom of Caspernos ]] under King Halstern the Cormyrus
**People**: Caspaners, Caspernians
**Demographic**: Humans; Dwarves; Halflings (some); Orcs (some); Elves (some)
**Capital**: Wyvernthor, the Sunset City

Prose (rendered markdown)

Session 4 · The Starfall

We reached Greywater Ford two days after the sky broke. Brannoc swore the light came down beyond the old ring, and the miller’s boy said the same, though he called it a lantern carried by nobody. Nobody has asked the Abbess of the Scarp yet. #session #starfall #greywater

What we found

  • A crater the width of a barn, the soil turned to black glass at the rim.
  • Three standing stones knocked flat; the fourth still upright and warm to the touch.
  • Tracks leading north toward the map: Ashen Scarp.

“The stones were never a wall. They were a lock.” — the miller, half asleep

Rules note

Touching the warm stone calls for a DC 13 Constitution saving throw; on a failure the creature has the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.

The star is not dead

Ysolde’s detect magic reading pulsed twice while we watched. Something inside is still awake.
